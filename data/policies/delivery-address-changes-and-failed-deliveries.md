# Delivery Address Changes and Failed Deliveries

Sometimes plans change after an order is placed, or a delivery attempt does not succeed. This policy explains how to update a delivery address, what happens when a parcel cannot be delivered, and when you may be charged for redelivery.

## Changing the Delivery Address

You can change the delivery address free of charge until the order is dispatched. After dispatch, the address can only be changed by the carrier, and only if the carrier offers a redirection service in your area.

To change an address before dispatch, open the order in your account and select "Edit delivery address". If the order has already been packed, the change request is sent to the warehouse, which may not be able to stop the parcel in time.

Address changes are not permitted to a different country. In that case, cancel the order and place a new one, or contact support.

## Failed Delivery Attempts

The carrier makes up to two delivery attempts on separate business days. A failed attempt is recorded when:

- No one is available to sign for a parcel that requires a signature.
- The address cannot be found or access is blocked.
- The parcel is too large for the letterbox or safe place specified.

After two failed attempts, the parcel is held at the nearest depot for 5 business days. You will receive a collection notice with the depot address and opening hours.

## Collecting From a Depot

Bring photo identification and the collection code from your notice. Depots are generally open until 19:00 on weekdays and until 13:00 on Saturdays. Parcels not collected within 5 business days are returned to our warehouse.

## Redelivery Fees

Redelivery is free for the first failed attempt. A redelivery fee of $6.50 applies when a parcel is returned to our warehouse and you ask us to send it again. The fee is waived if the failure was caused by the carrier or by a delivery error on our side.

## Returned to Sender Parcels

When a parcel is returned to our warehouse, we contact you to confirm whether you want a redelivery or a refund. If we do not hear from you within 10 business days, the order is refunded minus the return shipping cost. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## Safe Place Instructions

You may specify a safe place, such as a porch or garden shed, when placing your order. Our carriers follow these instructions where it is safe to do so. We are not liable for parcels left in a safe place that are later stolen or damaged, although we will help with the carrier's claim process.

## Contact

Address changes and collection questions should be sent to support via the Help tab with your order number. Please include the new address exactly as you want it recorded.
