# Rewards Tier Benefits

Northwind Rewards members move through three tiers as they spend. Each tier unlocks additional benefits on top of the standard points programme. This policy explains how tiers are assigned, what each one includes, and how tier status is kept or lost.

## The Three Tiers

| Tier | Qualifying spend (rolling 12 months) | Earn rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | Joining the programme | 1 point per $1 |
| Silver | $500 or more | 1.25 points per $1 |
| Gold | $1,500 or more | 1.5 points per $1 |

Bronze is the default for every member. Tiers are reviewed monthly, so a member may move up in the middle of a month once they cross a threshold.

## Silver Benefits

Silver members receive:

- Early access to sales events, 24 hours before the general public.
- Free standard shipping on all orders, with no minimum spend.
- A birthday discount code for 10% off a single order.

## Gold Benefits

Gold members receive everything in Silver, plus:

- Free express shipping on orders over $60.
- A dedicated support queue, with responses targeted within four business hours.
- Extended returns: 60 days from delivery instead of the standard window, applied to all eligible items.
- Quarterly bonus points equal to 2% of qualifying spend in the previous quarter.

## How Tiers Are Kept

Tier status is calculated on a rolling 12-month basis. A member keeps their tier while they remain above the threshold. Returns reduce qualifying spend, so a large return can lower your tier.

Gold members who fall below the Gold threshold keep Gold benefits for an additional 90 days as a grace period. After that, they move to Silver or Bronze based on their current spend.

## Tier Benefits and Returns

Extended returns for Gold members apply to the same categories as standard returns. Final Sale and clearance items are excluded. Return postage rules are unchanged for Gold members, except on defective items, which are always free.

## Tier Changes and Communication

We email you when your tier changes. Tier progress is shown on your Rewards page, including how much more you need to spend to reach the next tier.

## Corporate and Shared Accounts

Business accounts and accounts shared between household members do not qualify for tiers. Tier status is attached to the individual account holder only.

## Changes to Benefits

We may change tier benefits with 60 days' notice. Thresholds may be adjusted once a year, and we will publish the new values before they take effect.
