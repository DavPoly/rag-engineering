# Northwind Rewards Points Policy

The Northwind Rewards programme gives members points on every eligible purchase. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future orders. This policy explains how points are earned, how they are recorded, and the limits that apply.

## Joining

Membership is free. Create a Northwind account and opt in to Rewards from the account settings page. Members must be 16 or older. Accounts can hold only one Rewards membership.

## How Points Are Earned

Members earn **1 point for every $1** spent on eligible products, calculated on the amount paid after discounts and before tax and shipping. Points are credited when the order is delivered.

The following purchases do not earn points:

- Gift card purchases.
- Shipping charges, duties, and warranty plans.
- Orders paid entirely with gift card balance.
- Orders placed by a business account.

## Bonus Earning Events

From time to time we run promotions that offer double or triple points on selected categories. Promotional points are shown in your account as a separate line and follow the same expiry rules as standard points.

## Redeeming Points

Points can be redeemed at checkout in blocks of 100. Each block of 100 points is worth **$5** off the order total. Redemptions are limited to 50% of the order value. Points cannot be used on Final Sale items or on gift card purchases.

## Returns and Points

When an order containing earned points is returned, the points for the returned items are reversed. If you have already redeemed points on that order, the redeemed amount is recalculated against the refund. Any shortfall is deducted from your available balance.

## Balance Display

Your current balance, pending points, and expiry dates are shown on the Rewards page. Pending points appear for up to 30 days after an order is placed and are converted to available points on delivery.

## Account Closure

If a member closes their account, unredeemed points are forfeited. We do not refund points in cash or transfer them to another account.

## Abuse and Fraud

We may reverse points or suspend membership where we find evidence of fraud, such as returns used to exploit the programme, or the creation of multiple accounts. Members are notified before any action is taken where this is possible.

## Changes

We may change the earning rate or redemption value with 30 days' notice. Points earned before a change keep their original value.

Points expiry is covered in a separate policy. Tier benefits are described in the Rewards Tier Benefits policy.
