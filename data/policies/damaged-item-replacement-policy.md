# Damaged Item Replacement Policy

When a product fails within its early life, or arrives in a condition that makes it unusable, we replace it rather than making you wait for a repair. This policy sets out when a replacement is offered, how it is sourced, and what happens to the original item.

## When a Replacement Is Offered

We offer a replacement when an item:

- Arrives damaged or defective, as confirmed under the Damaged on Arrival Policy.
- Fails within 30 days of delivery because of a manufacturing fault.
- Is found to be missing a component that was included in the listing.

Replacements are not offered for items damaged by misuse, accidental breakage after delivery, or normal wear.

## Stock Availability

Replacements are sent from the same product line where stock allows. If the exact colour, size, or variant is out of stock, we offer one of the following options:

1. A replacement in a similar variant at the same price.
2. A replacement at a higher price, with the difference refunded to you.
3. A full refund.

You choose the option. We will not substitute a different product line without your agreement.

## Dispatch Timing

Replacements are dispatched within 2 business days of approval when the item is in stock. Where stock is on order, we give you a confirmed date before you accept the replacement. You may cancel for a full refund at any point before dispatch.

## Returning the Faulty Item

For most replacements, we send the new item first and ask you to return the faulty one within 14 days using the prepaid label. If the faulty item is not returned within 14 days, we may charge the replacement cost to the payment method on file. We will notify you before any charge is made.

For low-value items under $25, we do not require the faulty item to be returned.

## Serial-Numbered Products

For products with serial numbers, we record the serial number of the faulty item and the replacement. If the same product fails twice in the same way within 90 days, we escalate the case for a full refund rather than a second replacement.

## Multiple Replacements

Where the same item has been replaced twice, a third replacement is offered only after review by a senior member of the support team. This protects you from repeated failures and protects us from a product issue that needs wider investigation.

## Warranty Interaction

Replacements under this policy do not reset the warranty period on the original item. The warranty on the replacement runs from the date the replacement is delivered. See the Warranty Claims Process for repairs and warranty-period faults.
