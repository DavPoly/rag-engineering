# Failed Payments and Chargebacks

Payments do not always go through, and sometimes customers dispute a charge with their bank. This policy explains what happens when a payment fails, what you should do, and how we handle chargebacks.

## Failed Payments

A payment can fail for several reasons: insufficient funds, a card that has expired, a bank that declined the transaction, or a failed 3-D Secure check. When this happens, your order is held for 24 hours so you can try again.

If the payment is still unsuccessful after 24 hours, the order is cancelled automatically. No charge is made, and you receive an email explaining what happened.

## Retrying a Payment

To retry, open the order in your account and select "Retry payment", or return to your basket. You can use the same method or a different one. Retrying does not change the order's price, but stock is only held for the 24-hour window.

## Duplicate Charges

If you see two charges for the same order, one is usually a pending authorisation that will be released by your bank within 7 days. If both charges are still shown after 7 days, contact support. We will refund the duplicate within 5 business days of confirming it.

## Chargebacks

A chargeback is a dispute raised with your bank. We ask that you contact us before raising one, because most issues can be resolved faster directly. Chargebacks are costly for both sides and can delay refunds.

If a chargeback is raised, we receive a notice from your bank and our payment provider. We respond with the order details, delivery evidence, and any correspondence. The bank then decides the outcome.

## Impact of a Chargeback

While a chargeback is open:

- Your account is flagged, and new orders may require additional verification.
- Any refund for the same order is paused until the chargeback outcome is known.
- Rewards points for the disputed order are suspended.

If the chargeback is won by Northwind Goods, the funds are kept and the account returns to normal. If it is lost, the amount is debited from your account, and the order is closed.

## Friendly Fraud

Where we identify a pattern of chargebacks on orders that were delivered and not disputed by the customer, we may decline future orders from that account. We review each case individually and will explain the decision.

## Suspected Fraud on Your Account

If you see a transaction you did not make, contact your bank and then contact us. We will freeze the affected payment method on your account and review the order.

## Contact

For failed payment questions, use the Payments tab in your account. For a chargeback you have already raised, quote the case reference from your bank so we can respond quickly.
