# Accepted Payment Methods

Northwind Goods accepts a range of payment methods at checkout, both for individual customers and business accounts. This policy lists what we accept, how each method is processed, and the limits that apply.

## Cards

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Maestro. Cards must be issued in a currency we support at checkout. Debit and credit cards are treated the same way for purchases, except where your bank imposes its own rules.

Card details are processed by our payment provider. Northwind Goods does not store full card numbers on its own systems.

## Digital Wallets

The following wallets are available on most devices:

- Apple Pay on supported Apple devices and Safari.
- Google Pay on supported Android devices and Chrome.
- PayPal, subject to a verified PayPal account.

Wallet payments are subject to the same authorisation checks as card payments.

## Northwind Gift Cards

Gift cards can be used for full or partial payment. If a gift card does not cover the order total, the remainder is charged to another accepted method. Gift cards do not expire and are not redeemable for cash, except where required by law.

Multiple gift cards may be used on a single order, up to a maximum of five per transaction.

## Bank Transfer

Bank transfer is available for orders over $500 and for business accounts. Transfers must be made using the reference shown on your invoice. Orders are dispatched once funds clear, typically within two business days. Payments without the correct reference may be delayed.

## Payments Not Accepted

We do not accept cheques, cash on delivery, cryptocurrency, or buy-now-pay-later services. Store credit cannot be used as a payment method on new orders.

## Currency

Prices are shown in the currency of your delivery address. Your card issuer may apply its own currency conversion fees, which Northwind Goods does not control.

## Payment Security

Payments are protected by 3-D Secure authentication where your bank requires it. You may be asked to confirm a purchase in your banking app. If authentication fails, the order is not placed and no charge is made.

## Saved Payment Methods

You may save a card or wallet to your account for faster checkout. Saved methods can be removed at any time from the Payment tab in your account. Removing a method does not cancel open orders that were paid with it.
