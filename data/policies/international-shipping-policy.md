# International Shipping Policy

Northwind Goods ships to more than 40 countries from our European and North American hubs. This policy explains which destinations we serve, how duties and taxes are handled, and what to expect at the border.

## Supported Destinations

We ship to all EU member states, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. Other countries are shown as unavailable at checkout. We do not ship to P.O. boxes, military addresses, or countries under international trade restrictions.

## Shipping Rates

International shipping is charged by weight band and destination zone. Rates are shown before you pay. Orders over $150 to EU destinations qualify for free international standard shipping.

| Zone | Standard transit | Express transit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU and Switzerland | 4 to 7 business days | 2 to 3 business days |
| UK and Norway | 5 to 8 business days | 2 to 4 business days |
| Canada and USA | 5 to 9 business days | 3 to 5 business days |
| Australia and New Zealand | 8 to 14 business days | 4 to 6 business days |

## Duties and Import Taxes

Duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer unless the order was placed under our Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) option. Where DDP is offered, the duty amount is added at checkout and paid to us; we remit it on your behalf.

If you refuse a parcel because of unexpected duties, the goods are returned to us and refunded minus the return shipping fee and any duties already paid. We cannot refund duties that the destination country has already collected.

## Customs Documentation

We include a commercial invoice with every international parcel. Some countries require an additional declaration for certain product categories, such as skincare containing alcohol or electronic devices with batteries. Where this applies, the product page states it.

## Lithium Batteries

Products containing lithium batteries cannot be shipped by air to every destination. If your order is affected, we will split the shipment or switch to a surface carrier, and tell you the revised delivery window before dispatch.

## Delays at Customs

Customs holds are outside our control. If a parcel is held for more than 10 business days, contact support and we will provide the documents the carrier needs. We do not refund shipping costs for customs delays.

## Returns from International Addresses

International returns follow the Returns Policy, with one exception: the return shipping cost is borne by the customer, and the refund is reduced by that amount.
