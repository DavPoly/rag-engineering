# Final Sale and Clearance Returns

Some products at Northwind Goods are sold on terms that differ from our standard return process. This policy explains which items fall into that category and what options remain available to you.

## What Counts as Final Sale

An item is Final Sale when the product page or the cart clearly states "Final Sale", "Non-returnable", or "Clearance – no returns". Final Sale status is shown again on the order summary before you pay, so you can see it before committing.

Clearance items are discounted by at least 30% off the regular price. These items are sold to clear stock, so they are not eligible for return unless they arrive damaged or defective.

## Items Not Eligible for Return

- Final Sale and clearance apparel, footwear, and accessories.
- Personalised or engraved products.
- Digital goods, including gift cards and downloadable warranty extensions.
- Opened consumables such as candles, skincare, and food items.

## Options for Final Sale Items

If you change your mind about a Final Sale item, you can:

1. Resell it through a third-party marketplace.
2. Give it as a gift to someone who can use it.
3. Contact us within 7 days of delivery to ask whether an exchange for a different Final Sale product is possible. Exchanges are discretionary and not guaranteed.

## Damaged or Defective Clearance Items

Clearance items that arrive damaged or defective are fully covered. Report the problem within 14 days of delivery, with a photo of the damage and the order number. We will send a replacement of the same item, or a refund if the item is out of stock.

For more on damage claims, see the Damaged on Arrival Policy.

## Wrong Item Received

If we send you a different product from the one you ordered, including a Final Sale item in place of a standard item, this is our error. We will arrange free return shipping and send the correct item or refund you in full.

## Pre-Orders

Pre-orders marked "Ships on or after" follow the same rules as Final Sale items until the release date. Once the item has shipped, normal return rules apply based on the delivery date.

## Refund Method

Where a refund is due on a Final Sale item, it is paid to the original payment method. Store credit is available on request.

## Questions

Contact returns@northwindgoods.example with your order number. Our team responds within two business days on working days.
