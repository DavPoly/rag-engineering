# Warranty Coverage by Product Code

Every Northwind Goods product carries a warranty code and a SKU. Use this reference to find the warranty length, coverage type, and exclusions for a specific product. If you cannot find your product, use the warranty claims process instead.

## How to Read the Codes

- **SKU-#####** is the five-digit stock-keeping unit printed on the product box and the packing slip.
- **WR-####-X** is the warranty reference. The four digits identify the warranty family, and the final letter identifies the coverage tier: A is parts only, B is parts and labour, C is parts, labour, and accidental damage.

Warranty periods run from the delivery date shown on your order.

## Exclusions

Warranty does not cover:

- Damage from water ingress, unless the product is rated for it.
- Use with unapproved accessories or voltage converters.
- Cosmetic wear, scratches, or discolouration.
- Consumable parts, including filters, blades beyond their first 6 months, and batteries after 12 months.

Products with the WR-####-A tier do not include labour. Repairs carried out at a non-approved workshop void the warranty.

## Accidental Damage Tier

Tier C covers drops, spills, and power surges up to a single incident per 12 months. An excess of $15 applies to each accidental damage claim. Tier C is not available on products bought second-hand.

## Proof of Purchase

Keep your order confirmation. For warranty claims, we match the SKU on the order to the WR code in this table. If the WR code printed on your product does not match the table, contact support before submitting a claim.
