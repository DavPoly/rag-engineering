# Payment Refund Processing

Once a return is approved or a refund is agreed, this policy explains how the money moves back to you. It covers the refund route for each payment method, expected timings, and what to do if a refund does not arrive.

## Refund Routes

Refunds are returned to the method used for the original payment wherever possible:

- **Cards** are refunded to the card used at checkout.
- **Digital wallets** are refunded to the same wallet balance or linked card.
- **PayPal** refunds go to the originating PayPal account.
- **Gift cards** are refunded as a new gift card balance.
- **Bank transfers** are refunded by bank transfer to the account the payment came from.

Where the original payment method has expired or been closed, we contact you to arrange an alternative refund route.

## Timing

Refunds are initiated within 5 business days of the return being approved. Once initiated, the funds typically reach your account in:

| Route | Typical arrival |
| --- | --- |
| Visa and Mastercard | 3 to 7 business days |
| American Express | 3 to 10 business days |
| Digital wallets | 1 to 5 business days |
| PayPal | Same day to 3 business days |
| Bank transfer | 3 to 5 business days |

Your bank or card issuer controls when the funds are posted, so the final timing may vary.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds are issued when only some items on an order are returned, or when a deduction applies. Your refund email shows the breakdown of items, deductions, and the amount returned.

## Currency Differences

If the refund currency differs from the original payment currency, the amount is converted at the exchange rate on the refund date. This may be more or less than the original amount. We do not compensate for exchange rate movement.

## Refunds After Chargeback

If a chargeback has already been raised on the order, we will not issue a refund through the standard route. See the Failed Payments and Chargebacks policy for how these cases are handled.

## Refund Not Received

If the refund has not arrived within the timings above, first check your bank statement, including pending transactions. If it is still missing, contact support with the refund reference, which appears in your refund email. We will check the status with our payment provider and respond within three business days.

## Refund Fees

Northwind Goods does not charge fees for processing refunds. Where a card issuer charges a foreign transaction fee on a refund, this is determined by your bank.

## Records

Refund records are kept in your account for seven years, in line with our accounting obligations.
