# Standard and Express Shipping Policy

Northwind Goods ships orders from our regional distribution centres to customers across the mainland, and to selected islands and remote postcodes. This policy describes the domestic shipping options, what they cost, and how long they take.

## Shipping Options

| Option | Delivery window | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | Free on orders over $35; otherwise $4.95 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $12.95 flat |
| Same-day (selected cities) | Same calendar day if ordered before 11:00 | $9.95 |

Delivery windows start the business day after dispatch. Orders placed after 15:00 on a business day are dispatched the next business day.

## Order Processing

Every order passes a short fraud and address check before dispatch. Most orders pass within minutes. Orders that need manual review may take up to one additional business day. You will receive a dispatch confirmation with a tracking link once the parcel leaves our warehouse.

## Carriers

We use three carriers depending on destination and parcel weight. You cannot choose a carrier at checkout, but the tracking link always shows which one is handling your parcel.

## Parcel Size and Weight

Parcels up to 30 kg are eligible for standard and express shipping. Furniture and oversize items are shipped on a separate freight service with its own delivery window, which is quoted on the product page.

## Delivery Attempts

The carrier makes up to two delivery attempts. If neither attempt succeeds, the parcel is held at the nearest depot for 5 business days. For more information on address changes and failed deliveries, see that policy separately.

## Free Shipping Eligibility

Free standard shipping applies to the merchandise total after discounts, before tax. Gift card purchases, delivery fees, and returns postage do not count toward the free shipping threshold.

## Public Holidays

Delivery times are extended by one business day during national public holidays. Same-day delivery is not offered on public holidays.

## Questions

For questions about a specific delivery, use the tracking link in your confirmation email. For anything else, contact support via the Help tab in your account.
