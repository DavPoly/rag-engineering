# Transit Damage Claims Procedure

Damage that happens while a parcel is in the care of a carrier is treated differently from a fault in the product itself. This procedure explains how we handle transit damage claims, what evidence we need, and how long each stage takes.

## Scope

This procedure applies to any item that arrives with external packaging that is crushed, torn, wet, or otherwise compromised, and where the contents are damaged as a result. It also applies to parcels that arrive with visible signs of tampering.

## Step 1: Check Before Signing

Where possible, inspect the parcel before you sign for it. If the box is damaged, write "damaged packaging" on the delivery signature and keep the parcel unopened if you can. This note strengthens your claim considerably.

If you did not notice the damage until after signing, that is still acceptable. Note the date you discovered it.

## Step 2: Document the Damage

Within 48 hours of delivery, take:

- A photo of the external packaging from all sides, showing the shipping label.
- A photo of the damaged contents.
- A photo of any packing material inside the box.

Keep all packaging until the claim is closed. Carriers will often ask to inspect the original box.

## Step 3: Submit the Claim

Submit the claim through the Damage tab, selecting "Transit damage". Include your order number, the tracking number, and the photos. Claims submitted more than 14 days after delivery may be declined by the carrier, so please act promptly.

## Step 4: Our Handling

Once we receive a valid claim, we replace or refund the item within 3 business days. This happens before the carrier has reviewed the claim, so you are not kept waiting. We then file a claim with the carrier on your behalf.

## Step 5: Carrier Review

Carriers typically take 10 to 20 business days to review transit claims. You do not need to contact the carrier. If the carrier rejects the claim, we absorb the cost and do not pass it on to you.

## Shipping Costs

Shipping costs for the original order are refunded in full for transit damage. Return postage is free for any item that must be sent back for inspection.

## Repeated Claims

If three or more transit damage claims are raised on the same address within 90 days, we contact you to discuss a safe place or a collection point. This is intended to protect you from repeated delivery problems, not to refuse future orders.

## Fraud Prevention

We review claims for signs of fraud, including photos that have been edited or reused across orders. Claims that fail this review are referred to a senior team member within 2 business days.
