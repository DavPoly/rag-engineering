# Return Shipping and Refund Timelines

This document covers the mechanics after you decide to send an item back: how to get a return label, how to ship safely, and how long each stage takes. The eligibility rules for returns are set out in the main Returns Policy.

## Generating a Return Label

Return labels are created from the Returns tab in your account. Select the order, choose the items, and pick a reason. A prepaid label is emailed to you if the return qualifies for free shipping. Otherwise, the label is available at the flat rate shown during checkout.

Labels are valid for **21 days** from the date they are generated. If a label expires, request a new one from the same page; the old label is then cancelled automatically.

## Packing Your Return

- Use the original packaging where possible.
- Include the packing slip or print the return confirmation from your account.
- Remove any personal data from electronic devices before sending.
- Keep the tracking number until your refund has been issued.

Northwind Goods is not responsible for items lost in transit when a return was sent without tracking. We recommend a service with signature confirmation for items above $150.

## Drop-Off Options

Returns can be dropped at any partner carrier location, or collected from your address if you choose a pickup. Pickup is available for returns over $50 in a single parcel, subject to local availability.

## Timeline

| Stage | Typical duration |
| --- | --- |
| Label generated to parcel scanned by carrier | 1 to 3 business days |
| Carrier transit to Northwind warehouse | 3 to 7 business days |
| Inspection at warehouse | 1 to 2 business days |
| Refund issued after inspection | 5 business days |
| Funds visible on your statement | 1 to 5 business days |

Times are estimates and can lengthen during peak periods such as November and December.

## Partial Refunds

If an item is returned with missing parts or signs of use, we refund the value of the item less the cost of replacing the missing parts. You will receive an email explaining the deduction before any money is returned.

## Tracking Your Refund

Your account shows the current status of each return: label created, in transit, received, inspecting, or refunded. If the status has not changed for 10 business days after arrival, contact support with your return reference.

## Refunds to Gift Cards

Where the original order was paid with a Northwind gift card, the refund is issued as a new gift card balance. These balances do not expire.
