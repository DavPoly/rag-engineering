# Damaged on Arrival Policy

Every item leaves our warehouse packed and checked. If something arrives broken, cracked, or otherwise unusable, this policy explains how to report it and what we will do to put it right.

## What Counts as Damaged on Arrival

An item is damaged on arrival when it is physically broken, cracked, crushed, leaking, or missing a component that was included in the original listing, and the damage was present when the parcel was delivered. Cosmetic marks that do not affect function, such as light scuffing on a bag, are handled under the Returns Policy instead.

## Reporting Damage

Report damage within **14 days** of delivery. You can do this from the Damage tab in your account or by emailing damage@northwindgoods.example.

Please include:

- Your order number and the name of the affected item.
- At least two photos: one of the item and one of the outer packaging.
- A short description of what you found.

Please keep the packaging and any inner protection until your claim is resolved. We may ask you to return the item.

## Our Response

We aim to respond within 1 business day. Once your claim is approved, we will choose one of the following:

1. **Replacement** sent at no cost, dispatched within 2 business days if the item is in stock.
2. **Full refund** to the original payment method, including original shipping costs.
3. **Partial refund** for items where the damage affects only part of the product, such as a cracked lid on a set. The refund amount is confirmed in writing before it is issued.

## Returning the Damaged Item

For most claims we ask you to return the damaged item using the prepaid label that comes with your approval email. Return postage for damaged goods is free. Do not dispose of the item until you have been told the claim is closed.

For items under $20, we may waive the return and issue a refund or replacement straight away.

## Food and Perishable Items

Food, drink, and perishable goods must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Please keep the item and take a photo of its best-before date. Perishable claims are handled as refunds only.

## Declined Claims

We may decline a claim if the damage appears to have been caused after delivery, if the item was used in a way the product manual forbids, or if photographic evidence does not match the report. In these cases we will explain our reasoning and you can ask for a second review within 7 days.

## Carrier Damage

Damage caused in transit is covered under this policy, and we pursue the carrier on your behalf. See the Transit Damage Claims Procedure for how that works in practice.
