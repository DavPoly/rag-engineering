# Points Expiry and Redemption Rules

Northwind Rewards points do not last forever. This policy sets out when points expire, how activity keeps them alive, and the rules that apply when you redeem them, including how redemptions interact with returns.

## Expiry

Points expire after **12 months of account inactivity**. Inactivity means no completed order, no redemption, and no points-earning event on the account during that period.

Each batch of points is tracked by the date it was earned. If the account has been inactive for 12 months, all unredeemed points expire together on the anniversary of the last activity. We send a warning email 30 days before expiry.

## Keeping Points Alive

Any of the following resets the inactivity clock:

- Placing an order that earns points.
- Redeeming points at checkout.
- Completing a Rewards survey or review that we have flagged as eligible.

Simply logging in does not count as activity.

## Expiry of Promotional Points

Promotional bonus points earned during a campaign expire 90 days after they are credited, regardless of account activity. This is shown on each promotional line in your account.

## Redemption Blocks

Points are redeemed in blocks of 100. Each block is worth $5 off. Partial blocks cannot be redeemed, so if you hold 240 points, you can redeem 200 and keep 40 for later.

The order discount from points is capped at 50% of the order subtotal, before tax and shipping. Redemptions cannot be combined with a promotional code that already gives more than 20% off the order.

## Order of Redemption

When you redeem points, the oldest unexpired points are used first. This means you are less likely to lose points to expiry.

## Redeeming on Returns

If you return an order where points were redeemed:

- The redeemed amount is added back to the refund only if the refund is issued to the original payment method.
- If the refund is issued as store credit, the redeemed points are restored to your account instead.

Points earned on the returned items are reversed in full.

## Gift Card Purchases

Points cannot be redeemed against gift card purchases, and gift card purchases do not earn points.

## Restoring Expired Points

Expired points cannot be restored. Where we make an error in calculating expiry, we will correct the balance within 5 business days of your report.

## Questions

Your expiry dates are visible on the Rewards page. For questions about a specific batch, contact support with the order number that earned the points.
