# Shipping Delays and Lost Parcels

Most orders arrive on time. When they do not, this policy explains what we will do, how long you should wait before raising a concern, and what compensation is available.

## Delayed Orders

An order is considered delayed when the tracking status has not changed for 5 business days, or when the estimated delivery date has passed by more than 2 business days. You can check the status from the order page in your account at any time.

Delays are most common during peak sales periods, bad weather, and carrier strikes. We will email you when we become aware of a significant delay affecting your order.

## What To Do If Your Order Is Delayed

1. Check the tracking link in your confirmation email.
2. Wait for the window stated in the table below before contacting us.
3. If the window has passed, contact support with your order number.

| Shipping option | Wait before contacting us |
| --- | --- |
| Same-day | 1 business day |
| Express | 2 business days |
| Standard | 5 business days |
| International | 10 business days |

## Lost Parcels

A parcel is treated as lost when the carrier confirms it cannot be found, or when no scan has been recorded for 14 days after dispatch. Once an order is confirmed as lost, we will either send a replacement at no charge or refund the full order value, including shipping, at your choice.

Refunds for lost parcels are issued within 3 business days of confirmation. Replacements are dispatched within 2 business days.

## Stolen Parcels

If tracking shows the parcel was delivered but you did not receive it, report this within 48 hours. We may ask you to check with neighbours and to file a police report. Parcels confirmed as delivered to the correct address are not eligible for replacement, but we will review each case individually for orders above $200.

## Compensation

For delays longer than 7 business days on standard shipping, a credit of $5 is issued to your account automatically. This credit is not applied to orders over $300, which are handled through a case review.

## Carrier Responsibility

Our carriers are responsible for parcels in their custody. Where a carrier accepts liability, we pass the resulting claim on to you as a refund or replacement. You do not need to contact the carrier directly.

## Claims Timeline

Please report a lost or delayed parcel within 30 days of the expected delivery date. Claims received after 30 days may not be eligible for replacement, although we will still review refund requests in exceptional circumstances.
