# Returns Policy

Northwind Goods wants every customer to feel confident buying from us. This policy explains how to return an item, how long you have to do it, and what you receive back.

## Return Window

You may return most items within **30 days** of the delivery date shown on your shipping confirmation. The clock starts on the day the parcel is delivered, not the day you ordered.

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the packing slip or a valid order number.

## Eligible Items

- Home goods, kitchenware, and small appliances in original condition.
- Apparel and footwear that has not been worn outdoors and still carries tags.
- Electronics, provided the seal on the accessory box is intact.

## Restocking Fee

Opened electronics are subject to a **15% restocking fee** to cover inspection and repackaging. Unopened electronics are returned at full value. Home goods and apparel carry no restocking fee.

## Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs on all returns. We recommend a tracked service. Northwind Goods does not refund the original shipping charge on any order.

## Returns Without a Receipt

If you do not have your receipt, we can offer **store credit only** at the lowest price the item sold for in the previous 90 days. Cash refunds and original-payment refunds require proof of purchase.

## Refund Timing

Once the returned parcel reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, refunds are issued to the original payment method within **10 business days**. Depending on your bank, the funds may take an additional week to appear.

## Final Sale Items

Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase cannot be returned. Gift cards are non-refundable.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or colour are processed as a return followed by a new order. Price differences are charged or refunded at the time of the exchange.

## Contacting Support

Use the Returns tab in your account, or email returns@northwindgoods.example. Please include your order number and a photo of the item if it is damaged.
