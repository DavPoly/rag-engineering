# Extended Warranty Plans

Northwind Goods offers optional extended warranty plans that extend the standard manufacturer warranty on selected products. This policy describes the available plans, what they cover, and how to buy, cancel, or claim against one.

## Available Plans

| Plan | Extra coverage | Price (approx.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extend Plus | 12 additional months, parts and labour | 10% of product price |
| Extend Max | 24 additional months, parts, labour, and accidental damage | 18% of product price |

Plans are available for most appliances, electronics, and small home products bought from Northwind Goods within 30 days of delivery. Plans cannot be added to items bought second-hand, items sold as Final Sale, or products already past their standard warranty period.

## Buying a Plan

Extended warranty can be added at checkout or within 30 days of delivery from the order page in your account. The price depends on the product category and is shown before you pay. Plans are charged once and are not subscriptions.

## When the Plan Starts

The extended period begins on the day the standard warranty ends. You will not be charged twice for overlapping coverage. Your plan details, including the end date, are emailed to you and shown in your account.

## Claims Under an Extended Plan

Claims are submitted through the same process as standard warranty claims. The plan applies once the standard warranty has expired, or from the date of purchase where a product fault falls within the standard period and the standard warranty is found to be insufficient.

Excess charges are as follows:

- Extend Plus: $10 per repair.
- Extend Max: $15 per repair for accidental damage, nil for other faults.

## Cancellation and Refunds

You may cancel an extended warranty within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, provided you have not made a claim. After 30 days, cancellations are refunded on a pro-rata basis for each complete month remaining, minus a $5 administration fee.

## Transfer

Extended warranties are linked to the original purchaser. They can be transferred to a new owner once, if the purchaser contacts support with the new owner's details and proof of the transfer.

## Exclusions

Extended warranties follow the same exclusions as the standard warranty, including consumables and water damage. Accidental damage cover under Extend Max does not include damage caused by deliberate misuse or unapproved modifications.

## Questions

Plan questions can be sent via the Warranty tab in your account. Plans are administered on our behalf by a third-party provider, so claims decisions may take up to 15 business days.
