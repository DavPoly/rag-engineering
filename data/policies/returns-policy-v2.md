# Returns Policy

This policy replaces all earlier return policies for Northwind Goods. It applies to every order delivered on or after the effective date above. It explains how to return an item, how long you have to do it, and how refunds are issued.

## Return Window

You may return most items within **45 days** of the delivery date shown on your shipping confirmation. The window is generous on purpose: many of our products are gifts that take time to be opened and tested.

Items should be unused and returned in their original packaging, with the packing slip or a valid order number.

## Eligible Items

- Home goods, kitchenware, and small appliances.
- Apparel and footwear, including items that have been tried on indoors.
- Electronics, whether or not the accessory seal has been broken.

## Restocking Fee

We have removed the restocking fee. Opened and unopened electronics are refunded at full value, provided the device is complete and undamaged. Any device returned with missing accessories will be refunded minus the replacement cost of those accessories.

## Return Shipping

Return shipping is **free** for defective items, items shipped in error, and items returned within 14 days of delivery. For all other returns, a flat fee of **$6.95** is deducted from the refund. A prepaid label is available in your account.

## Returns Without a Receipt

Customers without a receipt may still return items. Refunds are issued to the original payment method when the order can be located by email address or phone number on the account. Otherwise, we offer store credit at the lowest price the item sold for in the previous 90 days.

## Refund Timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5 business days** of the item passing inspection at our warehouse. Most customers see the funds within three business days of that date.

## Final Sale Items

Clearance items and products labelled "Final Sale" are excluded from this policy. See the separate Final Sale and Clearance Returns policy for details. Gift cards are non-refundable.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are handled as a return followed by a new order. If the replacement costs more, you pay the difference at checkout. If it costs less, the difference is refunded within 5 business days.

## Contacting Support

Start a return from the Returns tab in your account, or email returns@northwindgoods.example. Attach a photo if the item is damaged so that we can prioritise your case.
