# Warranty Claims Process

If a Northwind Goods product develops a fault within its warranty period, this process explains how to make a claim, what we need from you, and what happens next. The warranty terms themselves, including coverage by product code, are set out in the Warranty Coverage reference.

## Before You Claim

Check the following first. Many issues are resolved without a claim:

- The product manual's troubleshooting section.
- Power, batteries, and connections.
- Whether a firmware or app update is available for connected products.

If the fault persists, start a claim.

## Starting a Claim

Open the Warranty tab in your account and select the order and product. Describe the fault, choose the most accurate category, and attach a short video or photos if relevant. Video is especially useful for noise, sparking, or intermittent faults.

You will receive a claim reference number within one business day. Keep this number for all follow-up.

## What Happens Next

1. **Assessment**: Our team reviews the claim within 2 business days and may ask a follow-up question.
2. **Decision**: The claim is approved for repair, replacement, or refund, or declined with a written reason.
3. **Fulfilment**: Approved claims are fulfilled within 10 business days where possible.

For products with the parts-only tier, labour charges are not included. The repair is carried out by an approved service partner, and any labour costs are shown in your approval email.

## Repair, Replacement, or Refund

Most faults within the first 12 months are repaired. Where a repair is not economical, or where the product cannot be repaired within 30 days, we offer a replacement of the same model or a refund. Products that fail three times for the same fault within the warranty period are replaced.

## Shipping Products for Repair

If the product must be sent to a service partner, we provide a prepaid collection label. Please remove any personal data and accessories not related to the fault. We are not responsible for accessories that are sent with the product but not returned.

## Declined Claims

A claim may be declined if the fault is caused by:

- Misuse, accidental damage outside the covered tier, or water ingress.
- Use with unapproved accessories or incorrect voltage.
- Unauthorised repairs carried out by a third party.

You may request a second review within 14 days of a declined decision by replying to the decision email with any additional evidence.

## Timeframes

Claims must be made within the warranty period. Repairs and replacements carried out under warranty do not extend the original warranty period, except that replacement items receive a new warranty from their delivery date.

## Contact

Use the Warranty tab for updates on your claim. For urgent safety issues, such as overheating or electrical smell, stop using the product immediately and contact support by phone at the number listed in your account.
